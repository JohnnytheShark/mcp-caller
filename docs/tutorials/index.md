# Tutorials

**Tutorials are learning-oriented.** They take you through a series of steps to complete a meaningful project. The goal is not efficiency — the goal is *learning*.

When you follow a tutorial, you are expected to not know things in advance. Everything you need will be explained.

---

## Available Tutorials

### [Getting Started — Your First MCP Tool Call](getting-started.md)
**Time**: ~10 minutes | **Requires**: Rust, Node.js

Connect to a real MCP server, discover its tools, fill in parameters interactively, and get your first tool result — all without an LLM.
